# 🚿 Bathroom / Shower Booking — User Guide

This app lets every housemate book the shared bathroom in advance so nobody clashes in the morning. Remember that ONLY ONE SHOWER per day is allowed.

---

## 1. Access the App

Open this link on your phone:

🔗 **[https://luisfernarndo1.github.io/uk/](https://luisfernarndo1.github.io/uk/)**

No download needed — it works directly in your browser, but even better you can save it on your home screen ( Read step 3 )

---

## 2. Create Your Account

1. Tap **✨ Sign Up**
2. Enter your name, email address, and a password (at least 6 characters)
3. Tap **Create Account**
4. Open your email inbox and click the **confirmation link** you'll receive
5. Come back to the app and tap **🔑 Log In** with the same email and password

> ⚠️ You must confirm your email before you can log in for the first time.

---

## 3. Install on Your Phone

You can add the app to your Home Screen so it opens like a regular app — no App Store needed.

### On iPhone
1. Open the link above in **Safari** (not Chrome or another browser)
2. Tap the **Share button** — the box with an arrow ↑ at the bottom of the screen
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Tap **Add** in the top right corner

### On Android
1. Open the link in **Chrome**
2. Tap the **three dots** in the top-right corner
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install App"**
4. Tap **Add**

📹 **Watch the iPhone walkthrough**: [YouTube Short ↗](https://youtube.com/shorts/_DLrFlrzuFc)

---

## 4. How to Book the Bathroom

1. Open the app and log in
2. Select **Today** or **Tomorrow** at the top
3. Tap the **＋ button** in the bottom-right corner
4. Choose your **start time** (or tap *Now* to use the current time)
5. Select a **duration**: 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 minutes
6. Tap **Confirm Booking**

If the slot you picked overlaps with someone else's booking, the app will warn you and ask you to choose a different time.

---

## 5. Cancel a Booking

Find your booking in the list and tap **Delete**. Only your own bookings can be deleted.

---

## Tips

- 💡 Book the night before so you don't have to rush in the morning
- 👀 You can always see what other housemates have booked — no surprises
- ⏰ Past bookings disappear automatically after a few days to keep things tidy
